The mechanism

The condominium

A territory jointly administered by two states without resolving final sovereignty.

Step 1

Demilitarized Special Administrative Zone

Step 2

Jointly overseen by Ukraine and Russia

Step 3

International monitoring and arbitration

Step 4

Sovereignty deferred; claims preserved

1

Brčko, Bosnia: placed under international supervision after the Yugoslav wars, precisely to defer the question of who owned it.

2

The New Hebrides: run jointly by Britain and France for decades before becoming Vanuatu. Imperfect precedents: neither involved a nuclear-armed belligerent.

Why the name matters

A name is a specification

“There are two hard problems in computer science: cache invalidation and naming things.”

1

It's told as a joke, but a name says what a thing is for before the thing exists.

2

The proposals on the table use bureaucratic language: demilitarized zones, civilian administrations, trade corridors. That gives neither side a story to tell its own people.

3

Each country ratifies by plebiscite, so the name is what each electorate will understand itself to be voting on.

THE NAME

👑

My preference

The case runs through Moscow

Kyivan Rus was the medieval state, centered on Kyiv, from which both Ukrainians and Russians trace their origins.

1

Putin did not justify this war in territorial terms. He justified it in civilizational ones: historical unity, one people, a shared cradle artificially divided.

2

It is the story he will eventually have to use to explain the casualties to his own country. A settlement named after a coal basin gives him no way to finish that story. A settlement named after Rus does.

3

Putin decides when Russia stops fighting, and a name that lets him stop may be worth more than it looks.

THE TEXT READ IN KYIV

THE TEXT READ IN MOSCOW

Киевская Русь

Authentic texts in both languages; each campaign runs in its own. And the name begins with Ukraine's capital.

1

Ukraine claims Kyivan Rus as its own heritage, the way Italy claims Rome, and acknowledges the shared East Slavic origin. What it rejects is Moscow's claim to be the rightful heir.

2

A condominium is a shared institution, and sharedness is closer to Moscow's version of the history than Kyiv's.

3

So Kyiv sells the substance instead: the fighting stops, the guarantees are real, the legal claim survives.

What a name can't do

Guarantees with real teeth

1

A condominium without credible deterrence is a new fault line waiting to produce a new war. An attack on the zone or across it has to make escalation irrational.

2

Without credible guarantors, it becomes what Minsk was: a piece of paper that buys time for the next invasion.

🇦🇪

UAE

Has already hosted trilateral negotiations and facilitated multiple prisoner exchanges: an obvious candidate

🇹🇷 🇮🇳 🇷🇸

Turkey · India · Serbia

Further possibilities, each bringing a different combination of diplomatic independence, military capacity and regional credibility

A naive first pass at guarantors acceptable to both sides; the Board of Peace could provide the scaffolding.
