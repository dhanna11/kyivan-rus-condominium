Stop at the line

The 28-point plan

Crimea, Luhansk and Donetsk recognized as de facto Russian, including by the U.S.; Ukraine withdraws from the rest of Donetsk.

Answered with land Russia hadn’t taken.

The 20-point draft

The line frozen in all four regions; by Kyiv’s account, the withdrawal and the recognition dropped; the Donbas left open.
