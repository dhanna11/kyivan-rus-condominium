The condominium

A territory jointly administered by two states without resolving final sovereignty.

Step 1

Demilitarized Special Administrative Zone

Step 2

Jointly overseen by Ukraine and Russia

Step 3

International monitoring and arbitration

Step 4

Sovereignty deferred; claims preserved

1

Brčko, Bosnia: placed under international supervision after the Yugoslav wars, precisely to defer the question of who owned it.

2

The New Hebrides: run jointly by Britain and France for decades before becoming Vanuatu. Imperfect precedents: neither involved a nuclear-armed belligerent.

3

The edge: there is no longer a single line on a map in the Donbas where one side's control ends. The contested gray zone could become the condominium's outer buffer.
